Home / About

A trusted name in global chemical & commodity trade

Founded to make international chemical and commodity trade simpler for businesses everywhere.

Maser Global Impex was founded with a single vision: to simplify international chemical and commodity trade for businesses worldwide. Over two decades we have built a network that spans Asia, Europe, the Middle East, Africa and the Americas.

Our team of trade professionals, regulatory experts and logistics specialists keeps every transaction moving, from first inquiry to final delivery. We don't just ship products. We build long-term partnerships.

From our base in Vancouver, BC, Canada, we work with buyers and sellers on both sides of the Pacific and beyond, with Pacific-gateway access through the Port of Vancouver.
